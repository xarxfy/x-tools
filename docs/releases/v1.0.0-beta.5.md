> **X-TOOLS is entirely “vibe coded”** (written with an AI assistant) and has had no independent security audit.
> This is a **beta** – install it on a **separate test VM or LXC (not on the Proxmox host)**, keep backups.

**Install** (as root, on a Debian/Ubuntu VM or LXC with internet access):

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xarxfy/x-tools/main/install.sh | sudo XT_REF=v1.0.0-beta.5 bash
```

**Update from an earlier beta:** `sudo x-tools-ctl update`. **Make a backup first** (*Administration → Backup*): this release changes the database (customers, tickets, tags) and cannot be rolled back.
**Remove:** `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xarxfy/x-tools/main/uninstall.sh | sudo bash`

### What is new
- **Customers as a small CRM**: a sortable table of customers (category, contact, services, servers, status, contract end, open tickets), filters by category and tag, and a customer page with tabs – several **contact people**, **contract data** (end, period of notice, support hours; the overview warns 60 days before the last day to give notice), **links**, and servers and jobs per customer.
- **Tickets**: per customer and in one list (*Tickets*) – status, priority, assignee, due date, related service or server, comments; every change is logged and the **history** of a customer collects comments, changes and free notes. Overdue, soon due and urgent tickets show on the overview and in the search.
- **Order** (*Administration → Order*): tags (one list for servers and customers), customer categories and server groups are managed in one place with eight colours; everywhere else you only pick from the lists.
- **PDF report** of your servers and services – for everything or for one customer; never contains credentials or notes.
- **Tables** no longer scroll sideways; on phones every row becomes a card and tab bars wrap.
- New permissions: *Work on tickets*, *Contract data*, *Manage tags and categories* (the last one for administrators only by default).

Full list: [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/xarxfy/x-tools/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md). The migration of existing customers, contacts and tags is tested, but please try the update on a copy first.
